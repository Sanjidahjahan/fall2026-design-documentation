# About Me

Hi! I'm Sanjidah, a senior studying Cognitive Science and Human-Computer Interaction at Barnard College, Columbia University. I love designing simple, intuitive experiences and thinking about how people interact with the things they use every day, usually on a screen.

This semester, I'm excited to take that thinking off the screen and into physical space. I want to see how designing with sensors, materials, and circuits changes how I approach interaction compared to designing an app. I'm especially curious what physical constraints can teach me about intuitive design that a digital interface can't.
